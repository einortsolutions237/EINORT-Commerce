## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (verbatim): *A merchant can list a product and a customer can complete a full purchase through to a merchant-confirmed order, with stock, order state, and payment-claim integrity guaranteed server-side.*

Derived user story (same derivation 03-01 through 03-05 committed):
**As a** Cameroonian merchant and their customer, **I want to** list products and complete a purchase through WhatsApp, Mobile Money claim, or cash on delivery, **so that** the sale is recorded, the stock is correct, and the payment status is auditable.

**This plan's slice:** the customer closes the loop. They send the money on their phone, come back to their link, tap `I've paid`, type the reference from their confirmation SMS and optionally attach the screenshot — and the merchant's nav badge turns gold within seconds. If the merchant rejects it, the same link tells them why and lets them send corrected details. This is the last unbuilt half of the manual-payment path, and with it the whole phase becomes a round trip.

<objective>
Build claim submission and resubmission: the unauthenticated token-gated screenshot upload path, the `submitClaim` action with its ORD-04 duplicate enforcement and its D-11 resubmission branch, the D-13 merchant email fired from `after()`, and the B6 form wired into the tracking page's `PAYMENT_PENDING` and `DISPUTED` action regions.

Purpose: CHK-04 is the customer's only way to tell the merchant they paid, and ORD-04 is the control that stops one transfer being claimed against two orders. Both meet in one action, and both have to survive a customer submitting twice on a bad connection.
Output: `src/server/claims/{submit,notify}.ts`, `src/server/images/claim-upload.ts`, the claim-finalize route, `claim-form.tsx`, the tracking-page wiring and one isolation test.
</objective>
