## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (verbatim): *A merchant can list a product and a customer can complete a full purchase through to a merchant-confirmed order, with stock, order state, and payment-claim integrity guaranteed server-side.*

Derived user story (same derivation 03-01 through 03-05 committed):
**As a** Cameroonian merchant and their customer, **I want to** list products and complete a purchase through WhatsApp, Mobile Money claim, or cash on delivery, **so that** the sale is recorded, the stock is correct, and the payment status is auditable.

**This plan's slice:** a merchant tells the platform where their money should arrive. Until this page exists, manual-transfer checkout has no receiving number to display and the WhatsApp path has no destination — D-14 says so in as many words. It also lands the three pure deep-link builders that D-15's research resolved, so the strings on the customer's phone are unit-tested rather than hoped for.

<objective>
Build the D-14 payment-settings surface (WhatsApp number, MTN receiving number + optional merchant code, Orange receiving number + optional merchant code, cash-on-delivery toggle) and the three pure `src/server/payments/*` string builders that CHK-02 and CHK-03 depend on.

Purpose: D-15 was this phase's research blocker. It resolved to a specific, narrow answer — MTN publishes `*126*4*<6-digit code>*<amount>#` and Orange publishes only `#150*47#` — and the only way that answer survives contact with a merchant's phone is if the string construction is a pure function with a test, not JSX interpolation.
Output: `src/server/payments/{phone,ussd,whatsapp,settings,actions}.ts`, the A6 page and its form, and three unit test files.
</objective>
