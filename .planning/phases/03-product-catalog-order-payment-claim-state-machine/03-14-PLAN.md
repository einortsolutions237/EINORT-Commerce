## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (verbatim): *A merchant can list a product and a customer can complete a full purchase through to a merchant-confirmed order, with stock, order state, and payment-claim integrity guaranteed server-side.*

Derived user story (same derivation 03-01 through 03-05 committed):
**As a** Cameroonian merchant and their customer, **I want to** list products and complete a purchase through WhatsApp, Mobile Money claim, or cash on delivery, **so that** the sale is recorded, the stock is correct, and the payment status is auditable.

**This plan's slice:** the customer gets somewhere to stand. Their unguessable link opens a page that always says exactly where their order is — sent, received, waiting for payment, being confirmed, confirmed, disputed or complete — and when they owe money, it tells them the number and the exact amount in text big enough to read at arm's length, with a copy button under their thumb. CHK-05 is absolute about this: there is no state in which the customer is left uncertain.

<objective>
Build the D-12 tracking page: the token-hash lookup with its indistinguishable miss, the exhaustive CHK-05 status map, and the B5 manual-transfer payment-instructions block with the D-15 three-tier tap-to-dial rendering resolved server-side from the User-Agent.

Purpose: the manual-transfer instructions are the highest-stakes block on the storefront — a wrong number or an unreadable amount is money sent to nobody. D-15's research resolved that manual copy is the default on every platform and a `tel:` button appears only in one narrow case, and this is where that answer becomes markup.
Output: `src/server/orders/tracking.ts`, the `/s/[slug]/order/[token]` route with its status block, payment instructions, copy field and skeleton, and two test files.
</objective>
