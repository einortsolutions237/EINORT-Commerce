## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (verbatim): *A merchant can list a product and a customer can complete a full purchase through to a merchant-confirmed order, with stock, order state, and payment-claim integrity guaranteed server-side.*

Derived user story (same derivation 03-01 through 03-05 committed):
**As a** Cameroonian merchant and their customer, **I want to** list products and complete a purchase through WhatsApp, Mobile Money claim, or cash on delivery, **so that** the sale is recorded, the stock is correct, and the payment status is auditable.

**This plan's slice:** the phase becomes trustworthy. Every gate runs together on one commit, every requirement points at a test that actually exists and actually passes, and a human walks the three checkout paths on real hardware — including the two things a test runner physically cannot check, which are what an iPhone and an Android dialer do with a USSD `tel:` URI.

<objective>
Run the phase gate: the full automated suite plus lint, typecheck and a production build; a requirement-coverage test that fails the build if any Phase 3 requirement's named proof artifact is missing; the resolution of 03-VALIDATION.md's map; and a blocking human walkthrough of the three checkout paths on real devices.

Purpose: 03-VALIDATION.md currently carries `nyquist_compliant: false` and every row marked as a Wave-0 gap. This plan is where those become facts. The manual rows exist because Apple's Phone app behaviour with `*` and `#` and a real Android dialer's handling of a pre-filled USSD string cannot be simulated in a test runner — and CHK-03 is the requirement that turns on exactly that behaviour.
Output: a requirement-coverage test, an updated and signed-off 03-VALIDATION.md, and a human sign-off on the three paths.
</objective>
