## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (verbatim): *A merchant can list a product and a customer can complete a full purchase through to a merchant-confirmed order, with stock, order state, and payment-claim integrity guaranteed server-side.*

Derived user story (same derivation 03-01 through 03-05 committed):
**As a** Cameroonian merchant and their customer, **I want to** list products and complete a purchase through WhatsApp, Mobile Money claim, or cash on delivery, **so that** the sale is recorded, the stock is correct, and the payment status is auditable.

**This plan's slice:** placing an order becomes a real, provable act — stock moves atomically, the order and its audit trail are written together, and the customer gets a tracking token that is the only key to their order. 03-12's checkout page is a form on top of this; if this is wrong, nothing above it can be right.

<objective>
Build the order-placement engine: the tracking-token mint/hash pair, the human-readable order number, idempotent stock hold and release, `placeOrder()`, and the two isolation tests that prove CAT-03 and TEN-08.

Purpose: `tests/isolation/stock-race.test.ts` is named in 03-VALIDATION.md as *the phase's single most important test*. Overselling is the failure a merchant cannot recover from — they have taken money for a unit that does not exist. It has to be a database guarantee with a concurrency test behind it, not a code review opinion.
Output: `src/server/orders/{tracking-token,order-number,stock,place}.ts`, `src/server/idempotency/cache.ts`, two unit tests and two isolation tests.
</objective>
