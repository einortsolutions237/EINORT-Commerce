## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (verbatim): *A merchant can list a product and a customer can complete a full purchase through to a merchant-confirmed order, with stock, order state, and payment-claim integrity guaranteed server-side.*

Derived user story (same derivation 03-01 through 03-05 committed):
**As a** Cameroonian merchant and their customer, **I want to** list products and complete a purchase through WhatsApp, Mobile Money claim, or cash on delivery, **so that** the sale is recorded, the stock is correct, and the payment status is auditable.

**This plan's slice:** a merchant's catalog becomes real — they open `/dashboard/products`, see everything they sell with its stock and visibility, and can take a product off the storefront and put it back. The write layer underneath is complete (options, images, categories, the plan cap), which is what lets 03-11's form be pure UI rather than UI plus a second server implementation.

<objective>
Build the whole catalog write layer — the product-count entitlement resolver, the pure slug and variant-matrix helpers, the four `merchantAction` mutations and the tenant-scoped read queries — and the A1 products list page that renders them.

Purpose: CAT-01 is the phase's entry point; nothing downstream (storefront catalog, PDP, cart, order line items) has anything to read until products exist. The carried-forward 02-CONTEXT.md D-07 product cap was registered-but-unenforced in Phase 2 and this is the plan that makes it real.
Output: `src/server/catalog/{slug,variant-matrix,queries,actions}.ts`, `productLimitFor()` in `plans.ts`, `/dashboard/products` with its loading skeleton, and three test files.
</objective>
