## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (verbatim): *A merchant can list a product and a customer can complete a full purchase through to a merchant-confirmed order, with stock, order state, and payment-claim integrity guaranteed server-side.*

Derived user story (same derivation 03-01 through 03-05 committed):
**As a** Cameroonian merchant and their customer, **I want to** list products and complete a purchase through WhatsApp, Mobile Money claim, or cash on delivery, **so that** the sale is recorded, the stock is correct, and the payment status is auditable.

**This plan's slice:** a shopper who has never heard of this platform lands on `{store}.einort.com`, sees real products, opens one, picks a size, and puts it in a basket that is still there tomorrow — with no account, no login and no form. That is the first half of CHK-01 and the first customer-facing page the project has ever had beyond a placeholder.

<objective>
Build the anonymous cart (opaque cookie plus Redis) and the two storefront browse pages — the B1 catalog grid and the B2 product detail page with variant selection and add-to-cart.

Purpose: CHK-01 requires browse, view, add and review without an account. Everything the customer does after this point (cart review, checkout, tracking) reads the same cart, so its shape — ids and quantities, never money — is the decision that keeps TEN-08 true four plans later.
Output: `src/server/cart/{cache,actions}.ts`, `src/server/storefront/queries.ts`, the catalog and PDP routes with their loading skeletons, and two test files.
</objective>

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Anonymous internet -> storefront pages | Every visitor is untrusted and unauthenticated by design (CHK-01) |
| Cart cookie -> tenant | A cookie is the only thing tying a returning visitor to their basket |
| Stored cart -> displayed money | The cart is client-triggered state; the amounts must not come from it |
| Product slug in the URL -> another tenant's catalog | The tenant comes from the host, but the slug comes from the visitor |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-03-45 | Information disclosure | One cart cookie shared across every tenant's storefront | mitigate | The cookie is set with no `domain` attribute so it is host-scoped, and `StoredCart.tenantId` is compared against the host-resolved tenant on every read, discarding a mismatch |
| T-03-46 | Tampering | A forged `variantId` entering the cart | mitigate | `addToCart` reads the variant through `scopedDb(tenant.id)` and refuses an id that is missing, inactive or foreign, before the line is written |
| T-03-47 | Tampering | Prices carried in the cart and echoed back at checkout | mitigate | `StoredCart` has no money field; `hydrateCart` derives every amount from database rows; acceptance criteria grep for a price key returning 0 |
| T-03-48 | Information disclosure | Probing whether a product slug exists in another tenant | mitigate | `getStorefrontProduct` is tenant-scoped and the page renders the same `notFound()` for a foreign slug, an inactive product and a nonexistent one |
| T-03-49 | Denial of service | A scripted flood of `addToCart` filling Redis with abandoned carts | accept | 30-day TTL bounds retention, the payload is two fields per line and a quantity ceiling caps growth; order placement — the expensive path — is rate-limited by `orderPlacementLimiter` in 03-12 |
| T-03-SC | Tampering | npm installs | accept | No package installs in this plan |
