## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (verbatim): *A merchant can list a product and a customer can complete a full purchase through to a merchant-confirmed order, with stock, order state, and payment-claim integrity guaranteed server-side.*

Derived user story (same derivation 03-01 through 03-05 committed):
**As a** Cameroonian merchant and their customer, **I want to** list products and complete a purchase through WhatsApp, Mobile Money claim, or cash on delivery, **so that** the sale is recorded, the stock is correct, and the payment status is auditable.

**This plan's slice:** the merchant side of an order becomes usable — they open `/dashboard/orders`, see what came in through each channel, confirm a WhatsApp or cash-on-delivery sale with one tap, mark it fulfilled when it ships, and open any order to read exactly who moved it and when. That last part is ORD-05 stopping being a database table and becoming something a human can read.

<objective>
Build the merchant order surfaces: the tenant-scoped list and detail queries, the two `merchantAction` transitions, the one shared order-state chip module, the A3 list with its six filters, and the A4 detail page with the ORD-05 audit trail.

Purpose: D-02 makes WhatsApp and cash-on-delivery orders two-state — placed, then confirmed by the merchant — so without this page those two channels have no completion path at all. The chip module exists once because a state label inlined in two files is a state label that will disagree in three months.
Output: `src/server/orders/{queries,actions}.ts`, `src/components/order-state-chip.tsx`, the orders list and detail routes, and two test files.
</objective>
