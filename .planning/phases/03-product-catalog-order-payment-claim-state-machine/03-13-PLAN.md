## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (verbatim): *A merchant can list a product and a customer can complete a full purchase through to a merchant-confirmed order, with stock, order state, and payment-claim integrity guaranteed server-side.*

Derived user story (same derivation 03-01 through 03-05 committed):
**As a** Cameroonian merchant and their customer, **I want to** list products and complete a purchase through WhatsApp, Mobile Money claim, or cash on delivery, **so that** the sale is recorded, the stock is correct, and the payment status is auditable.

**This plan's slice:** the merchant becomes the judge. A claim arrives, the nav badge turns gold, they open the queue, see the amount and the reference and the screenshot side by side with the order total, and either confirm the payment in one tap or reject it with a reason the customer can act on. This is the human step that ORD-02 says can never be automated, and it is the only thing standing between a self-reported "I've paid" and a confirmed sale.

<objective>
Build the merchant side of the payment-claim loop: the pure reference normalizer, the review queries, the three `merchantAction` mutations (confirm, reject with a required reason, reopen), and the A5 card queue with its one-tap confirm and its reject dialog.

Purpose: ORD-03 asks for one-tap confirm/reject, ORD-02 forbids auto-confirmation, D-11 makes the rejection reason mandatory and the state recoverable, and D-04 requires the held stock to go back on sale on rejection. All four meet in this one surface, and getting the stock release wrong here silently oversells the merchant's inventory.
Output: `src/server/claims/{reference,queries,actions}.ts`, `/dashboard/claims` with its card, reject dialog and skeleton, and two test files.
</objective>
