# Phase 05.1 Plan 06: Picker Grid & Card Redesign Summary

**Container-query grid engine (Tailwind v4 `@container`/`@lg`/`@4xl`) replaces the viewport-breakpoint bug on `TemplatePicker`, with segment-grouped headings, a full-bleed 16:10 preview frame, and the `previewUrl`-vs-`TemplateThumbnail` D-05 fallback branch — pinned by a new 13-assertion source-scan contract test.**

## Accomplishments
- Deleted the exact bug the phase exists to fix: `grid grid-cols-2 gap-4 sm:grid-cols-3` (viewport breakpoints inside a fixed ~288px editor rail, yielding ~85px cards) is gone, replaced by a `@container` wrapper per segment group and `grid grid-cols-1 gap-6 @lg:grid-cols-2 @4xl:grid-cols-3`.
- Every template now renders grouped under a named, counted segment heading (`role="group"` + `aria-labelledby`, never `<h3>`), partitioned (not filtered) from the flat `tiles` array — the merchant's chosen industry is hoisted first on onboarding and titled "Recommended for you".
- Cards are now full-bleed with a `relative aspect-[16/10]` image frame; when `tile.previewUrl` is `null`, the frame silently falls back to `<TemplateThumbnail>` via a new optional `className` prop that neutralises its portrait aspect/min-width/double border inside the landscape card slot.
- Phase 5's tier-lock and inert-current-card controls (T-05-32, T-05-35) survive unchanged, including the `onValueChange` guard kept byte-identical.
- `tests/unit/template-picker-contract.test.ts` was written and observed RED against the shipped grid before the redesign, then GREEN after — including a positive control (the exact deleted string) and a negative control (the container-query replacement) on the viewport-breakpoint offender predicate.

## Decisions Made
- Grid spacing (group-to-group `gap-8`) lives on the single `RadioGroup`'s own `className`; per-group column spacing (`gap-6`) lives on each group's inner grid div — keeps exactly one `RadioGroup` for arrow-key traversal across segment boundaries while still expressing two different spacing steps at two different levels.
- `disableHoverLift` (locked OR current) is tracked separately from `isInert` (locked OR retained-above-tier) — a plain unlocked "current" card must omit the `group` class (no hover lift) without being radio-`disabled`, since re-selecting it is blocked by the `onValueChange` early-return, not by the disabled attribute.
- Segment headings are `<div id=...>` rather than `<span>`, since the UI-SPEC's "full grid width" requirement needs block-level layout; `role="group"`/`aria-labelledby` on the wrapper carries the required group semantics without promoting the heading to a real `<h3>`.
