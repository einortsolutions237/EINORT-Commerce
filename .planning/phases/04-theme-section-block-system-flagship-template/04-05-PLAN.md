<objective>
Close ONB-03's logo half: mint a logo upload, derive it without the product-photo enhancement chain, and let the finalize route accept it — without giving the client any say in which namespace or which Sharp preset runs.

Purpose: Phase 3 deliberately left `IMAGE_PRESETS.logo` and `UploadKind = "logos"` present and unused, with comments saying so in as many words. Three hardcoded call sites are all that stand between them and a working logo upload. The enhancement chain is the substantive change: `.normalise()`, `.modulate({ saturation })` and `.sharpen()` are tuned for an under-lit phone photo of a product, and on a flat brand logo they shift the brand's own colours, halo flat edges, and — with lossy WebP — fringe semi-transparent edges.

Output: a per-preset `enhance` flag, `requestLogoUpload`, a widened finalize schema with a server-side `kind -> preset` map, and unit cases for all three.
</objective>

<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Browser -> `requestLogoUpload` Server Action | Untrusted body; the Zod schema is the entire list of what a browser may influence |
| Browser -> `POST /api/upload/finalize` | A Route Handler, reachable by direct POST, outside `merchantAction`'s reach |
| Uploaded bytes -> R2 -> Sharp | Arbitrary attacker-supplied image bytes reach a native decoder |

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|

| T-04-18 | Tampering | A client-chosen Sharp preset | mitigate | `KIND_PRESET` is a server-side `Readonly<Record<…>>` map from namespace to preset, never a cast of `parsed.data.kind`. A third `UploadKind` becomes a compile error at the table. Pitfall 7 verbatim. |
