## Phase Goal

ROADMAP `**Goal:**` for Phase 6, verbatim: *A merchant can run their business day-to-day from a dashboard that surfaces what needs attention, and the platform owner can operate and support the pilot fleet of stores from a pilot-scoped Super Admin surface — including a direct messaging channel to every merchant and the ability to verify their subscription payments through it.*

**This plan's vertical slice:** a merchant opens `/dashboard` and can answer "what needs attention" and "how many products are live" without digging — the two clauses of DASH-01/DASH-02 that quick task `260906-egn` left unsatisfied.

<objective>
Extend the shipped Overview with a "Needs your attention" band and a fifth metric card. Rebuild nothing.

Purpose: 06-RESEARCH.md § Discretion Q3 read the shipped Overview line by line. "How is the business performing" is satisfied; "what needs attention" is not — low stock does not exist anywhere in the codebase, disputed orders are surfaced nowhere, and the pending-claims signal is a sidebar badge, which is a count rather than the work. This plan closes exactly those gaps.
Output: two new query functions, one exported threshold constant, one new band component, a fifth metric card, and matching loading skeletons.
</objective>
