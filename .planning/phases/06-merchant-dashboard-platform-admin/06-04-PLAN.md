## Phase Goal

ROADMAP `**Goal:**` for Phase 6, verbatim: *A merchant can run their business day-to-day from a dashboard that surfaces what needs attention, and the platform owner can operate and support the pilot fleet of stores from a pilot-scoped Super Admin surface — including a direct messaging channel to every merchant and the ability to verify their subscription payments through it.*

**This plan's vertical slice:** the owner logs in and lands on a real Platform Admin surface that visibly announces itself; everybody else gets a 404 that is indistinguishable from a route that never existed.

<objective>
Build the `/admin` shell — Surface C — as chrome and composition over the existing token system, and amend the gold-accent budget contract test in the same commit that spends the gold.

Purpose: 06-UI-SPEC.md's opening warning is that this phase adds a surface, not a palette. The shell is where that is either honoured or lost. It is also where D-06's "no local not-found.tsx" and Pitfall 8's "toasts do not exist on the admin surface" are either designed in or discovered later.
Output: three new components, the admin layout, the amended gold budget, and the D-03 deviation recorded in the comment that predicted otherwise.
</objective>
