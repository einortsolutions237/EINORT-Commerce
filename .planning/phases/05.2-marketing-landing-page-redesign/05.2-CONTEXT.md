**Gathered:** 2026-09-07

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

Replace the current bare-bones root page (`src/app/page.tsx` — wordmark, one tagline, one CTA, explicitly documented in its own header comment as "deliberately NOT a marketing site — that is future scope with its own phase") with a real, professional public marketing/landing page for EINORT-Commerce, modeled on the quality bar of Shopify's own marketing site (shopify.com), triggered by the user supplying a screenshot of that exact page and asking for the same standard.

Explicitly NOT in scope: the `/signup` flow, the onboarding flow, or the dashboard — this phase is the root `/` page only, the merchant's very first impression before they ever click through to create a store.

</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Trust-signal strategy
- **D-01:** No fabricated or borrowed social proof. Lead with concrete, real differentiators instead of a "powering millions of businesses" claim EINORT cannot honestly make: real Cameroon-first payment methods (Mobile Money, Orange Money, Cash on Delivery), a real browsable template gallery, and a genuine minutes-not-months setup story. No invented merchant counts, no fake logos, no borrowed testimonials.

### Visual asset strategy
- **D-02:** The hero visual is a real screenshot of an actual EINORT storefront template — not a custom illustration or generic stock graphic. **Sequencing dependency:** this depends on Phase 05.1 (Template Preview Rendering & Picker Redesign) actually landing its real-screenshot generation pipeline (currently Wave 1 of 5 complete as of this writing). Research and UI-SPEC for this phase can proceed now, but the plan's hero-visual task should either (a) be sequenced to execute after 05.1 completes, or (b) use a clearly-temporary placeholder (e.g., one of 05.1's already-approved segment placeholder photos, or the existing 05-09 CSS-wireframe thumbnail) with an explicit, tracked follow-up to swap in the real screenshot once 05.1 ships. The planner must decide and document which approach it takes — do not silently ship a permanent placeholder as if it were final.

### Language for the page
- **D-03:** English, matching the rest of the product. The entire product (dashboard, onboarding, storefront) is English-only today (confirmed: no French strings, no locale/language infrastructure anywhere in the codebase) — a French or bilingual landing page would be the one inconsistent surface, and real localization is out of this phase's scope.

### Page structure & length
- **D-04:** A focused, single-scroll page: hero, 3 key differentiator sections (payment methods / template quality / speed-to-launch), a simple "how it works" (pick industry → customize → publish), and one final CTA. Not a full Shopify-length page — sections like testimonials or broad pricing comparisons don't have honest content to fill yet and are deliberately excluded, consistent with D-01.

### Claude's Discretion
- Exact copywriting (headlines, body copy, CTA wording) — informed by deep research into what makes SaaS/e-commerce-builder landing pages convert, and by the project's existing brand voice (see canonical refs below).
- Exact visual layout, spacing, and component choices within the existing merchant-platform blue/gold/slate design system (see canonical refs) — this page should extend that system, not invent a new one, since it is the same brand a merchant will see moments later in onboarding.
- Whether/how to show the 3 differentiator sections visually (icons, small illustrations, screenshots) — left to the UI-SPEC step.
- Exact "how it works" step count and framing.

</decisions>
