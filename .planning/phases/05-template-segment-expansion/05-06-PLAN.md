## Phase Goal

Merchants outside the fashion segment get their own structurally distinct storefront, and the template library reaches 50 visually distinct variations (10 Starter / 15 Business / 25 Professional) that a stranger would not mistake for one another.

> Planner note: the ROADMAP `**Goal:**` line for Phase 5 is not in `As a … I want to … so that …` form. It is reproduced verbatim rather than invented. Run `/gsd mvp-phase 5` if the user-story framing is wanted.

<objective>
Add the `trust-bar:strip` and `contact:card` variants behind two-arm exhaustive switches, keeping each section type's existing settings shape, schema caps and accessibility floor unchanged.

Purpose: D-02's distinctiveness budget depends on these two section types contributing independent structural choices — the reachable skeleton space is `3 × 2 × 3 × 2 × 2 = 72` structural combinations with all five sections present, and dropping either of these two variants halves it. `05-UI-SPEC.md` § Background-treatment classification also depends on `trust-bar:strip`'s hairline-only treatment existing, since it is what lets a strip sit next to a white section without violating the anti-flatness rule.
Output: four files — two switches, two new variant components.
</objective>
