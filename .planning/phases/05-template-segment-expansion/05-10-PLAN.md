## Phase Goal

Merchants outside the fashion segment get their own structurally distinct storefront, and the template library reaches 50 visually distinct variations (10 Starter / 15 Business / 25 Professional) that a stranger would not mistake for one another.

> Planner note: the ROADMAP `**Goal:**` line for Phase 5 is not in `As a … I want to … so that …` form. It is reproduced verbatim rather than invented. Run `/gsd mvp-phase 5` if the user-story framing is wanted.

<objective>
Close the render path: thread a complete variant map from the stored template key through the renderer to every section component, on the live storefront and inside the editor's preview canvas.

Purpose: this is the plan that makes the previous three visible. Until it lands, the seven new variant components exist but nothing selects them. `05-RESEARCH.md` Finding 2 establishes the one discipline that must not be lost — the renderer reads `variants.hero`, a LITERAL key, never `variants[section.type]`, because a computed index widens to the union of all variants and forces the exact cast `section-renderer.tsx` exists to refuse. Pattern 8 establishes the other: the preview route is deliberately ungated and serves only published data, so the draft variant map travels by postMessage instead.
Output: the renderer's new prop, the two page call sites, and the fourth validated postMessage field.
</objective>
