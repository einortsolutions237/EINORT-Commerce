## Phase Goal

Merchants outside the fashion segment get their own structurally distinct storefront, and the template library reaches 50 visually distinct variations (10 Starter / 15 Business / 25 Professional) that a stranger would not mistake for one another.

> Planner note: the ROADMAP `**Goal:**` line for Phase 5 is not in `As a … I want to … so that …` form. It is reproduced verbatim rather than invented. Run `/gsd mvp-phase 5` if the user-story framing is wanted.

<objective>
Turn TMPL-05's "genericness is a failure condition" from a stated intention into a red build, and generalize Phase 4's single-template drift guard to all 50.

Purpose: 50 templates is 1,225 unordered pairs. Nobody performs 1,225 side-by-side comparisons, and a gate nobody performs is a gate that passes vacuously — precisely the failure `theming-registry.test.ts` already warns about at length for one template. The automated metric is also what makes the human layers in plan 05-22 tractable: it selects the six adversarial pairs by construction rather than by sampling, which is what makes sampling sound here and would not make random sampling sound.
Output: one new unit suite and one generalized existing suite, both DB-free and running in milliseconds.
</objective>

Create `tests/unit/template-distinctiveness.test.ts` in the DB-free `unit` project.

Define a `signatureOf(key)` helper returning three fields:

- `structure` — the ordered `"{type}:{variant}"` join of `TEMPLATES[key].sections`
- `accent` — `templateDefaultTokens(key).primaryAccent`, lowercased
- `voice` — the template's hero `eyebrow` and `heading` joined with a separator, read from `templateDefaultDocument(key)`

Assert all eight rules. Name the test blocks so the `-t` filters in `05-VALIDATION.md` match: at least one block name contains `segment` and at least one contains `nested`.

1. **No two templates share both `structure` and `accent`.** The literal definition of the same shop twice. Failure message names the FIX (change one template's variant assignment or its accent pair) and the WRONG FIX (do not relax this check — genericness is the failure condition).
2. **No `structure` is used by more than 2 templates.** This makes "25 skeletons" a tested claim rather than a plan.
3. **All 50 `voice` values are distinct.** Copy is one of the three distinctiveness axes; duplicates mean it was not really authored.
4. **All 50 keys are distinct and `Object.keys(TEMPLATES).length === 50`.** TMPL-04's literal count.
5. **Every segment has at least one Starter-accessible template.** The dead-end guard: a Starter merchant who picks an uncovered segment at onboarding opens a picker with nothing designed for them.
6. **The 10 Starter-accessible templates span at least 8 distinct structures.** Starter is the tier most merchants land on, so a concentrated Starter set makes the whole library read as generic to the majority of the product's users.
7. **Cumulative accessible counts are exactly 10 / 25 / 50 by tier and the sets are nested** — Starter ⊆ Business ⊆ Professional asserted by set containment, not by counting alone. Also assert the counts match `PLANS[tier].limits.templates`, with `professional` being `null`. That is the drift guard against the registered catalog size.
8. **Non-vacuity positive control.** Build a hand-made colliding pair inline — two synthetic signatures sharing structure and accent — and assert the comparison helper reports them as colliding. Inherit the idiom from `theming-registry.test.ts` lines 200-216. Without this, a bug in `signatureOf` makes every other rule pass while checking nothing.

Additionally, compute and emit the **six closest pairs** ranked by the metric: same `structure` first, then smallest numeric distance between the two `accent` values across their hex channels. Write them out in a form the plan summary can quote verbatim — plan 05-22's adversarial-pair stranger test consumes this ranking, and producing it here is what makes that sampling sound rather than arbitrary. Emitting the ranking must not itself fail the suite.

Every rule is objectively decidable and must run with no database, in the `unit` project, in milliseconds.
