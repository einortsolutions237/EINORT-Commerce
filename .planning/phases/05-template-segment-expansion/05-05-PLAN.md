## Phase Goal

Merchants outside the fashion segment get their own structurally distinct storefront, and the template library reaches 50 visually distinct variations (10 Starter / 15 Business / 25 Professional) that a stranger would not mistake for one another.

> Planner note: the ROADMAP `**Goal:**` line for Phase 5 is not in `As a … I want to … so that …` form. It is reproduced verbatim rather than invented. Run `/gsd mvp-phase 5` if the user-story framing is wanted.

<objective>
Turn the single hero component into a three-variant family — `full-bleed` (unchanged Phase 4 design), `split`, and `stack` — so a template's variant choice produces a structurally different above-the-fold, not a recolour.

Purpose: D-02 requires distinctiveness to come from rendering variants, because pure reordering of identically-rendered sections was explicitly rejected as too weak to pass TMPL-05's stranger test. `05-RESEARCH.md` Pitfall 4 names the way this fails: a variant designed against a stock mock-up, shipped with `backgroundImageKey: null`, that shows every merchant an empty band on day one. `hero:stack` is specified with no image slot at all precisely so it cannot fail that way.
Output: three hero variant components behind one exhaustive switch.
</objective>
